## What blocks a lossless generic stage (information dropped or rewritten with no v1 carry-through)

1. **`ontology_table_row:applyEncounterMap`** emits only the 8 listed measures, the relation and the window. Every other column is dropped, including `EncounterIdentifier` (by design, called "derived"). The source row is not kept; its id moves to the first emitted body. A missing onset or offset becomes **0** (`colNum` default). Units are `''`. Contrast the patch-geometry map, which carries unlisted columns.
2. **`image_stack` fold arm** has `if ~isempty(tableRowId) … end` with an empty body. The v1 `document_id` edge to the ontologyTableRow is not carried; the comment says "#88 dropped … the row is the NDI second pass's to resolve". This contradicts the header's "testImageStackFoldCarriesDocumentId…" text.
3. **`position_metadata`** reads only `ontology_node`. The v1 `units` and `dimensions` are not carried.
4. **`jCalculation` / `jTuningCurve` / `jContrastSensitivityValue`**: unmapped source fields go to `reportNote('field_dropped', …)` only (the `dropped` lists at jTuningCurve ~141-259 and 388-489).
5. **`stimulus_response_scalar`**: `stimulus_response.stimulator_epochid` and the `stimulus_control_id` edge are dropped, with a note only.
6. **`jDoseValue`** vehicle placeholder: the amount is sent to a report note and `chemicals(k).source_value/source_unit` are cleared.
7. **`ethicsJurisdiction`**: an unrecognised `EthicsAssessment` produces no statement (note only).
8. **`foldGenericFiles`**: `date_created` / `date_updated` are dropped (counted as `date_fields_dropped`); `pyramid_type` is dropped.
9. **`resolveOpenmindsCitations`** (and `metadata_editor`, per its comment): an author's 2nd and later affiliations are dropped (counted).
