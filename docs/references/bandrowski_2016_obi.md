# Bandrowski et al. 2016: the Ontology for Biomedical Investigations (OBI)

Bandrowski A, Brinkman R, Brochhausen M, Brush MH, Bug B, Chibucos MC, et al.
"The Ontology for Biomedical Investigations." PLoS ONE 11(4): e0154556 (2016).
doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0154556.
<https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0154556>

Licence: Creative Commons CC0 public domain dedication (stated on the first
page). The copy here, `bandrowski_2016_obi.pdf`, is unmodified; it was
committed at the repository root as `file.pdf` (did-schema #22, 2026-05-12)
and renamed here.

Why it is here: OBI is the model V2 follows for describing how an
investigation was carried out (planned process, assay, device, specimen), and
OBI terms fill many V2 vocabulary slots (`schemas/registry/did_v2_term_registry.json`).
