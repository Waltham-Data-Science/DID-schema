**Q1 (#160, carry-096).** A v1 `ontologyLabel` labels a document that has no subject or no time reference. Example: a label on an image of an E. coli plate. No term observation of a subject can be written, so today it is carried as v1 (`awaiting_home`). Should this be (a) a `needs_mapping` carry, where each dataset's map says what the label is about, or (b) a generic V2 home, such as a term assertion about the labelled document's own entity? **Recommend (a).** The generic stage cannot know what the label describes ([one correct reading]), and the E. coli case is already a Haley workbook item ([E. coli stacks are a dataset decision]).

**Q2 (#159, carry-095).** A v1 `ontologyLabel` with a field other than `ontology_node`, or an edge other than `document_id`, is carried whole. Should this row split the way the tuning rows did ([tuning stray fields are malformed]): a field outside NDI's ontologyLabel template goes to carry-100 (malformed), and carry-095 keeps only real template fields that have no home? **Recommend yes.** Then carry-095 lists those template fields by name, and each gets its own home question.

**Q3 (#158, carry-092/094).** A v1 openMINDS `Strain` with fields the strain `entity` does not write is carried whole. Examples: `breedingType`, `stockNumber`, `diseaseModel`, `digitalIdentifier`, `alternateIdentifier`, `lookupLabel`. A `Species` or `GeneticStrainType` fragment with a definition or synonyms is also carried. Should these take the same treatment as [openMINDS subject leftovers]? That would mean:
- `stockNumber`, `digitalIdentifier` and `alternateIdentifier` go to the strain entity's `global_identifier`;
- `breedingType` and `diseaseModel` become term assertions on the strain;
- `lookupLabel` is notation (rebuilt);
- fragment definitions and synonyms are not stored, because they are read back from the term.

**Recommend yes.** It matches the subject-document rule decided today.

**Q4 (#157, carry-090).** A `control_stimulus_ids` whose presentation became a standalone item (no subject), or a Hartley frame sequence, is carried. [control pairing is a calculation] makes the pairing "a calculation about the presentation".
